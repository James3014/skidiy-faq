Coach Information and Teaching Languages

Is there a teaching style or evaluation of the coach available for reference?

ID: faq.general.029

Answer

You may refer to:

Coach's personal introduction page: includes certifications, philosophy, style, photos, and videos

Facebook group: You can join "Skidiy Japan Self-Guided Skiing Discussion Group" to view students' shared experiences and coach reviews

Teaching records: Some coaches will include links to teaching videos

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
