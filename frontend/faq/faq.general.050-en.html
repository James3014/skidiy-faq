What coaches are available for selection? What criteria can be used for filtering?

ID: faq.general.050

Answer

You can check the available instructors and fees on the website: Booking system 🔗

Instructor introduction page: Instructors 🔗

Filter conditions include:

* Ski/Snowboard
* Class location (e.g., whether in Kagura)
* Available booking slots (half-day or full-day)

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
