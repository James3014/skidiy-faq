Refund Policy Guidelines

What happens if the weather is bad on the day of the class after the deposit has been paid?

ID: faq.refund_policy.063

Answer

The decision is based on whether the ski resort is open: if the ski resort is closed, a refund will be issued; as long as the cable car is running, classes will proceed as normal in snow, rain, or cold weather.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
