Coach and Teaching Arrangements

How many students can a coach teach at most?

ID: faq.general.018

Answer

To ensure teaching quality and safety, one instructor can teach a maximum of 4 students. If there are more than 4 students, it is recommended to open additional classes or divide them into groups.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
