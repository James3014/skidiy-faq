Children's Skiing and Safety Assurance

At what age can one start learning to ski?

ID: faq.general.009

Answer

It is recommended to start from 3 years old and above. For children under 5 years old, one-on-one instruction is suggested to enhance safety and concentration.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
