Can I contact the coach in advance?

ID: faq.general.027

Answer

Your coach will usually contact you proactively seven days before the course. If you wish to be contacted earlier, you can also note your request when booking, and we will assist with the arrangements.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
