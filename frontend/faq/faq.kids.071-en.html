Children's Skiing and Safety

What are the safety mechanisms for children in class?

ID: faq.kids.071

Answer

Each coach has a maximum group size; insurance is included throughout the entire process; adults are arranged to accompany children when riding the cable car; coaches have experience in teaching children.

Postscript

Safety on the day is the top priority.
