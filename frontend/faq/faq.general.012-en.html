Children's Skiing and Safety Assurance

What are the coverage and limitations of children's insurance?

ID: faq.general.012

Answer

No death benefit for individuals under 15 years old, providing NT$200,000 in actual expense medical coverage and US$10,000 in emergency assistance compensation.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
