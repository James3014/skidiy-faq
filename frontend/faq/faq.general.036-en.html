General Booking and Scheduling

Can we add more people on site?

ID: faq.general.036

Answer

Yes. It is recommended to book with the estimated number of people from the beginning, which is beneficial for insurance arrangements and cost calculations. The website can calculate discount offers for different numbers of people.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
