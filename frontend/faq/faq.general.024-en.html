What languages does the coach speak?

ID: faq.general.024

Answer

* Chinese: All coaches are Taiwanese and fluent in Chinese.
* English: All possess basic English teaching ability, some can teach entirely in English or Cantonese.
* Thai: Currently not available.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
