🛟 Safety and Insurance

Does the course include insurance? What is the coverage?

ID: faq.general.054

Main Answer

SkiDIY courses include Fubon Insurance "New Happy Travel+" policy, valid for 3 days starting from the first day of class.

Don't forget travel insurance! Besides accident injury coverage, the insurance also includes:

  1. Travel accident insurance
  2. Accident medical
  3. Sudden illness
  4. Mobile phone theft
  5. Food poisoning
  6. Emergency rescue SOS compensation USD 10,000
  7. Diversion to non-original airport
  8. Flight delay
  9. Baggage delay
  10. Home theft, home fire, etc.

Fubon Policy Coverage

  • Death and disability (accident): TWD 2 million coverage (excluding children under 15)
  • Medical expense reimbursement (accident): TWD 200,000 coverage
  • Emergency rescue SOS compensation: USD 10,000
  • Under 15 years old: Only medical expense reimbursement TWD 200,000

Important Notes

⚠️ Cannot purchase additional Fubon travel insurance if enrolled in SkiDIY student insurance (New Happy Travel+)

For insurance details, please refer to Fubon official website.
