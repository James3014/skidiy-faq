Safety and Insurance

Does the course include insurance? What is the scope of coverage?

ID: faq.general.054

Answer

SKIDIY courses all include Fubon Ski Project Insurance, valid for four days from the first day of the course. The coverage is as follows:

* Accidental death and disability: 1 million (not applicable to those under 15 years old)
* Accidental medical actual expenses: 300,000
* Under 15 years old: 200,000 + 10,000 USD for emergency rescue

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
