Platform Use and Reservation Process

What should I do if I accidentally closed the page after clicking the payment link, or if the link has expired?

ID: faq.general.046

Answer

Our payment page is protected, and the link is time-sensitive. If you leave the page without completing the payment, the "unestablished" order will be automatically canceled in about 20 minutes, and your originally selected time slot will be released. At that time, you just need to return to the website and make your desired reservation again.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
