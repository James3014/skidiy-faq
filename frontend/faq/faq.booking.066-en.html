Can I cancel the appointment? Are there any refund policies?

ID: faq.booking.066

Answer

Cancellations are permitted, with charges based on the time of cancellation and policy. Please check your order page for status and available actions; if you need assistance, please contact customer service.

Postscript

For detailed refund procedures, please refer to the refund policy page.
