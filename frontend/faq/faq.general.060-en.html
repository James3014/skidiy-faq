What is the claim process if I seek medical treatment overseas?

ID: faq.general.060

Answer

The platform will provide assistance and guide you to contact the insurance window. You will need to prepare the following claim documents:

* Overseas diagnosis certificate, medical receipts, discharge summary (for inpatients)
* Passport entry and exit stamps/proof, National Health Insurance reimbursement documents

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
