Can adults and children take classes together?

ID: faq.grouping.008

Answer

Adults and children can attend classes together, but if the children are younger, the coach will prioritize ensuring their safety and basic movement practice, which may relatively reduce the adults' practice time. If you wish for adults' learning progress to be unaffected, it is recommended to arrange separate classes for children under 5 years old.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
