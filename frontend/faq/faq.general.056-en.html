Safety and Insurance

How to confirm if the insurance is complete? Will I receive the policy?

ID: faq.general.056

Answer

The insurance application process will be completed approximately one week before the class starts. Individual insurance policies will not be sent out. You can log in to the official website, go to "Account → Order Information" to fill it out, or use the dedicated form link we provide. You can check the insurance completion status of all students in "Order Information."

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
