I registered an account, but I haven't received the email verification letter. What should I do?

ID: faq.general.047

Answer

If you haven't received the verification email, please first check your 'spam' folder carefully. If it's not in your spam folder either, it's likely that you have previously registered with the same email. Please try using the 'Forgot Password' function to reset your password and log in. If the problem persists, please provide your registered email so that customer service can assist you with the inquiry.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
