Children's Skiing and Safety Assurance

Can parents accompany or film their children during class?

ID: faq.general.014

Answer

Yes. However, parents must maintain an appropriate distance from the coach to avoid affecting the teaching or causing safety concerns. If filming is necessary, please be mindful of your own and others' safety.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
