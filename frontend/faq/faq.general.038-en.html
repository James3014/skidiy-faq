Will the first day's class teach how to get on and off the cable car?

ID: faq.general.038

Answer

It depends on the on-site situation and learning progress. If feasible, it is recommended to purchase single-ride cable car tickets to accommodate flexible needs.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
