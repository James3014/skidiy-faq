Safety and Insurance

Is the insurance included in the course fee mandatory? If I have already purchased my own travel insurance, can I opt out or get a refund?

ID: faq.general.055

Answer

Yes, the Fubon Ski Project Insurance included in the course fee is provided to protect all students and is part of the course, so it cannot be canceled or refunded separately. This insurance mainly covers accidental injuries that occur during skiing activities. Please note that if you have already purchased Fubon's travel insurance, you may not be able to be covered by our project due to the insurance company's "double insurance" regulations, but the course fees and content will remain unchanged.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
