Coach and Lesson Arrangements

Why do some coaches have a 'minimum N lessons' booking restriction? Can I book just one lesson?

ID: faq.general.017

Answer

Some coaches, especially those who need to travel from a more distant area to the ski resort, may set a minimum number of lesson hours for appointments to cover their travel and time costs. This is a personal setting of the coach, and our platform respects the coach's teaching conditions. Therefore, if you wish to book this coach, it is recommended that you meet their minimum booking requirements, or choose another coach without this restriction.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
