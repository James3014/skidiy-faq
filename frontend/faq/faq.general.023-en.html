Does the coach have experience teaching children or the elderly?

ID: faq.general.023

Answer

Yes, our coaches have rich experience in teaching children and are also familiar with the learning pace of the elderly. For students with significant differences in learning progress, it is recommended to have separate classes to ensure teaching quality.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
