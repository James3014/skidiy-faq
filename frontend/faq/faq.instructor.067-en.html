Can I specify or recommend a coach? How do I choose a suitable coach?

ID: faq.instructor.067

Answer

You can specify a coach in the reservation system, or choose according to your skiing level and preferences. It is recommended to browse the coach list and introduction page first, which includes certifications, styles, and available teaching times.

Postscript

Popular coaches recommend booking early.
