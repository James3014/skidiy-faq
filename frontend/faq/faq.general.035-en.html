General Booking and Scheduling

Does a successful reservation mean it's confirmed?

ID: faq.general.035

Answer

The system will send a successful appointment notification email. Coaches usually confirm within 3 days; if the system coordinates with other coaches, you may need to wait about two weeks.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
