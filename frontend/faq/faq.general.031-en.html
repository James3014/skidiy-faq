General Booking and Scheduling

After successfully booking, can I change the date or time of the class?

ID: faq.general.031

Answer

To change the date or time of a confirmed lesson, please contact our customer service as soon as possible. We will inquire with your original instructor to see if they are available at the new time. If the instructor can accommodate, we will modify your order. If the instructor cannot accommodate, we will try to arrange another instructor for you, or you may choose to process your original order according to our cancellation policy.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
