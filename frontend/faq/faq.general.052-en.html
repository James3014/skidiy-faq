Platform Use and Reservation Process

What notifications will I receive after booking a course? Email or SMS?

ID: faq.general.052

Answer

* Booking successful notification email
* Insurance and class reminder notification email
* You can also check your order on the website at any time: My orders 🔗

Q: What do I need to prepare before class? Will the platform remind me?

A: Recommended preparations:

* Snow gear (snowboard, snow boots) can be rented in advance
* Helmet and protective gear (crash pants, knee, wrist guards) are recommended to be brought by yourself
* Basic equipment such as snow jacket and pants, gloves, and snow goggles
* Lift ticket: Some ski resorts offer discounted ticket purchase links
* Personal arrangements such as air tickets, accommodation, and transportation

Reminder methods:

* The coach will proactively contact you to confirm 7 days before class
* The system will send out a reminder email and insurance notification form

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
