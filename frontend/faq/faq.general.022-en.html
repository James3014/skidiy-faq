Does the coach have an international ski instructor certification? Which country issued it?

ID: faq.general.022

Answer

Yes, all our coaches hold international snow sports certifications, such as:

* New Zealand Snowsports Instructors Alliance (NZSIA)
* Canadian Association of Snowboard Instructors (CASI)
* Professional Ski Instructors of America (PSIA)

We require our coaches to possess valid certifications and participate in regular upgrades, such as Level 2 or Level 3 examinations.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
