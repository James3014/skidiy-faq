Children's Skiing and Safety

At what age can one start learning to ski? What are the precautions?

ID: faq.kids.069

Answer

Recommended for ages 3 and up; for children under 5, one-on-one instruction is suggested to enhance safety and focus. If attending with other children, an adult familiar with skiing must accompany them.

Postscript

Actual arrangements are subject to safety and teaching evaluations on the day.
