How to contact the coach after applying for the course?

ID: faq.general.028

Answer

After a successful booking, the system will send a notification email. The coach will contact you using the primary contact information on the order and confirm the meeting location and time before the class. Please ensure your email is correct and that you can be reached.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
