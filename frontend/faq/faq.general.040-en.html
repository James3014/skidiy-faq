Pricing and Payment

What does the course fee include? Does it include ski equipment rental and lift tickets?

ID: faq.general.040

Answer

The course fee includes coaching and ski insurance. Lift tickets (ski passes) and equipment rental fees are at your own expense. Some ski resorts offer student discounts and early bird ticket links; it is recommended to check in advance. Goggles and gloves at some ski resorts must be purchased and cannot be rented.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
