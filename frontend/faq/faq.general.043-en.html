Is the payment made directly to the coach or through the platform?

ID: faq.general.043

Answer

* Deposit: Collected by the platform via online credit card payment.
* Final Payment: Paid to the coach in Japanese Yen cash on-site.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the booking system.
