Coach Info and Contact

Can I contact the coach in advance? How do I communicate with the coach?

ID: faq.instructor.068

Answer

The coach will usually contact you proactively about 7 days before the course to confirm the meeting point and class details. If you need to be contacted earlier, please specify in the remarks, and we will assist with the arrangements.

Postscript

Please ensure the order contact information is correct and usable.
