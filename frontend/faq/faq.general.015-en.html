Coach and Teaching Arrangements

I want to take classes for several consecutive days, can I book different coaches?

ID: faq.general.015

Answer

Yes, but we highly recommend trying to stick with the same coach for your lessons. This is because the coach best understands your learning progress and situation, and can provide continuous guidance, which will lead to the best learning outcomes. You can 'specify a particular coach' in the booking system's filter to view all their available time slots.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
