Can different ages be in the same class?

ID: faq.general.010

Answer

It is not recommended to mix age groups such as 3, 5, and 7 years old in the same class, as there are significant differences in physical strength and learning progress. It is suggested to separate them for classes.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
