Platform Use and Reservation Process

How do I meet the coach on the day of the class? Is there a location or navigation?

ID: faq.general.053

Answer

* The coach will proactively contact you about a week before the class to inform you of the meeting location and time.
* Information on meeting locations will be published in the ski resort description on the website.
* If you do not receive contact, you can proactively contact customer service or the platform for assistance in relaying the message.
* The meeting location for each ski resort is slightly different; it is recommended to reconfirm with the coach.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
