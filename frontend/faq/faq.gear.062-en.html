What ski equipment is essential? Can you provide a list?

ID: faq.gear.062

Answer

* Available for rent: snowboard, boots, helmet
* Recommended to bring your own: goggles, gloves, snow jacket/pants, crash pants, knee pads
* Other preparation reminders: Please arrange air tickets, passport, transportation, lift tickets, and accommodation in advance.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
