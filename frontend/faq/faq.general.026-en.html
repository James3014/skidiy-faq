Can I request a specific coach?

ID: faq.general.026

Answer

Yes. You can choose a specific coach when making a reservation, and it is recommended to book popular time slots in advance. If you have no particular preference, the system can arrange a suitable coach for you.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
