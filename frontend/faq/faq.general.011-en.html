Children's Skiing and Safety Assurance

What safety mechanisms are there for children in class?

ID: faq.general.011

Answer

Each coach can guide a maximum of 6 people, ensuring the proportion of children is not too high; comprehensive ski-specific insurance is included throughout; coaches have experience teaching children, and adults will accompany children when using the cable car.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
