Coach and Lesson Arrangements

I already know how to ski, do I still need to take a beginner's class?

ID: faq.general.020

Answer

If you haven't skied in a while or are unsure of your skills, it's recommended to take a beginner's lesson first so an instructor can assess and adjust.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
