Platform Use and Reservation Process

What is the appointment booking process? How many steps are involved?

ID: faq.general.048

Answer

The reservation process varies depending on whether a coach offers a class.

* If a coach offers a class, you can directly book and complete your order on the website.
* If no coach offers a class, you need to apply for a class and wait for the coach's approval.
* After a successful reservation, you will receive a notification email, and subsequent information such as insurance and class reminders will also be sent to you.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
