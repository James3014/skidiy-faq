Platform Use and Reservation Process

Will the booking be confirmed if the coach cannot confirm it immediately?

ID: faq.general.051

Answer

* If the coach offers a class, the reservation can be confirmed immediately.
* If you need to apply for a class, the coach will respond within 2 days whether they accept.
* If the coach cannot accommodate, the system will inquire with other coaches. If none can accommodate, the order will be canceled and refunded.
* For specific ski resorts like Karuizawa, reservations require staying at Prince Hotel. If this condition is not met, the reservation may be canceled.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
