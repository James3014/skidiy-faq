Can I get a refund if I can't attend class after an injury?

ID: faq.general.058

Answer

Okay. Please provide a diagnostic certificate, and we will assist you with withdrawing from the course. The refund amount will be processed after deducting insurance fees and credit card handling fees.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
