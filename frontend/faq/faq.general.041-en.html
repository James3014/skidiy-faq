Pricing and Payment

Do I need to pay in full first? Can I just pay a deposit?

ID: faq.general.041

Answer

When booking a course, only a deposit needs to be paid online by credit card; the remaining balance can be paid in Japanese Yen cash to the instructor on the day of the lesson.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
