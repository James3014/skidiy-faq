What do children need to prepare for class?

ID: faq.general.013

Answer

Snow jacket, snow pants, gloves, ski socks, beanie, goggles, protective gear (hip pads, knee pads, wrist guards), sunscreen, warm clothing, water bottle, snacks. Layered dressing (onion style) is recommended for easy on and off. Snow gear (snowboards, ski boots) can be rented at the ski resort.

Postscript

For more information and the latest availability, please refer to the reservation system.
