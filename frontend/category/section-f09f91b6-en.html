At what age can one start learning to ski?

It is recommended to start from 3 years old and above. For children under 5 years old, one-on-one instruction is suggested to enhance safety and concentration.

Can different ages be in the same class?

It is not recommended to mix age groups such as 3, 5, and 7 years old in the same class, as there are significant differences in physical strength and learning progress. It is suggested to separate them for classes.

What safety mechanisms are there for children in class?

Each coach can guide a maximum of 6 people, ensuring the proportion of children is not too high; comprehensive ski-specific insurance is included throughout; coaches have experience teaching children, and adults will accompany children when using the cable car.
